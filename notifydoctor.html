NotifyDoctor privacy policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

NotifyDoctor is developed by Andrea Ferendeles. It helps you understand notification-delivery settings on your Android device.

Data processed on your device

When you explicitly enable Android notification access, NotifyDoctor processes only the metadata needed for local diagnostics: the source app package and display name, notification time, notification channel identifier, and delivery state. It does not store notification titles, message text, people, images, or other notification content.

Raw diagnostic event metadata is automatically deleted after 24 hours. Aggregated daily counts may remain on your device to show reliability trends. You can delete all app data from Android Settings at any time.

Sharing and selling data

NotifyDoctor does not send diagnostic data to the developer or to third parties, does not sell personal data, and does not use accessibility services. Diagnostic data remains on your device.

Permissions

Notification permission is used only for NotifyDoctor's own guidance and status notifications. Notification-listener access is optional and can be revoked at any time in Android Settings. The app explains the purpose of that access before you enable it.

Advertising and purchases

The app does not serve advertising. NotifyDoctor Pro is an optional one-time purchase processed by Google Play. The app uses the Google Play purchase result only to unlock Pro on your device; it does not send purchase tokens to the developer. Google handles payment information under its own privacy policy.
